<p>The 4/5 years that your child goes through in their Secondary years are
years of exploring new interests, developing deeper friendships and skills.
They are growing from a child to a young adult. They will develop their
own beliefs and convictions. These crucial years, the children needs a
significant adult in their lives to listen, care for, guide and be there
for them in a non-critical and non-judgemental way. Building a strong and
healthy relationship between parent and child is so crucial in these developmental
years.</p>
<p>Parents need to have knowledge and be equip with the skills to better
engage their children regularly, having deep one on one conversations.
Parenting Journey should be intentional, anchored on knowledge and skills
and not experimental. Learn from parents who have journeyed those years
with their children before.</p>
<p>The school has lined up several Parent workshop sessions. We hope you
will seize the opportunity to equip yourself as well as share your experiences
to the other parents</p>
<p>Parenting Workshops this year. The workshops are held on Saturdays from
9am.</p>

<p><strong>Parents’ Talk 1 August 2026</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Title</strong>: Understanding and Relating to your Teenagers:
Theory, Practice and Lived Experience</p>
<p><strong>Audience</strong>: For parents only</p>
<p><strong>Speaker</strong>: Dr Gloria Kong</p>
<p><strong>Synopsis</strong>:</p>
<p>Journeying with teenagers can be both challenging and rewarding. Parents
are faced with rapid changes in the physical, emotional, behavioural, and
social states of their teenagers, as well as the constant turmoil in identity
development accompanying adolescence. This workshop examines these changes
that teenagers experience, helping parents comprehend their teenagers’
stage of development, and their occasional puzzling actions and reactions.
It highlights the importance of effective communication, active listening,
and empathy as tools for building strong, trusting relationships. Parents
will find guidance on recognising the balance between granting independence
and providing necessary support, fostering self-esteem, resilience, and
promoting healthy decision-making. Ultimately, we aim to empower parents
with insights and approaches that not only enhance their relationship with
their teenagers but also support their growth into confident, responsible
individuals. The speaker, together with her spouse, will share their own
experience bringing up three very different children in their family.</p>
